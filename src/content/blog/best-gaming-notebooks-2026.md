## Why a Gaming Notebook is Different

A notebook sitting on your gaming desk needs to earn its place. 
It has to look right next to your keyboard, your controller, your monitor.
A plain ruled notebook from a stationery shop does not cut it.

That is exactly why Slatebooks exists.

## The GTA Series

The GTA series draws from Liberty City — dark covers, urban typography, 
the feeling of a night drive through the city. 

If your setup is dark and minimal — black desk, RGB off, matte everything — 
the GTA series belongs there.

## The Red Dead Series

Rugged. Warm. Textured kraft covers with earth tone stitching.
RDR2 notebooks are for the player who prefers a warmer desk aesthetic.
Wooden accents, warm lighting, mechanical keyboards in tan or brown.

## How to Pick the Right One

Ask yourself one question — what does your desk look like at night?

Dark and cold → GTA Series  
Warm and textured → Red Dead Series  
Clean and minimal → Cyberpunk Series
